Frequently asked questions

Who is HomeProof for?

Homeowners, renters and families who want to maintain organized records of their household possessions.

Is this an insurance company?

No. HomeProof is a household inventory and record management tool. It does not sell insurance or guarantee that an insurer will accept a particular report.

Do I pay monthly?

No. Our planned Basic and Premium packages use one-time payments of $10 and $20 respectively.

Can I access HomeProof on my phone?

Yes. The application is being designed to work on smartphones, tablets and computers through a browser.

Does HomeProof guarantee my data will never be lost?

No digital service can guarantee that. We are building secure storage and backup capabilities, and users should retain their own copies of important records.
